Colaba to Bandra: Historical Journey

schedule 45 min

Welcome to this journey across Mumbai. As we start near Colaba, you can feel the sea breeze off the Arabian Sea. Yeh shaher hamesha chalta rehta hai, just like the waves hitting the tetrapods at Marine Drive.

We are now heading towards Fort area. Look at the magnificent Gothic architecture around you. The Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus is a prime example. In 1887, when it was built, it symbolized the imperial power, but today it is the beating heart of local transport.

As we move further north along the coast, we cross the iconic Sea Link. The Bandra-Worli Sea Link is not just an engineering marvel; it’s a bridge between the old island city and the sprawling suburbs. Raat ke waqt, the cables light up, creating a beautiful silhouette against the sky. Let's talk about the history of Bandra next.

Bandra, historically known as Bandor, was a collection of fishing villages. The Portuguese influence here is still very visible in the names of streets and the beautiful old churches like Mount Mary.

Before the reclamation projects, you had to take a ferry to cross the Mahim creek to reach Bandra from the main island of Bombay. Can you imagine that? Aaj toh we just zip across in minutes.

As we conclude this segment, notice the shift in architecture from the colonial stone buildings of South Mumbai to the more eclectic, modern, and sometimes chaotic structures of the suburbs.
